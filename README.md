# Selected Advanced Topics in Mathematical Analysis

### A Rigorous Treatment of Selected Chapters from the Mathematical Analysis Curriculum

**Author:** Murad Qasımov  
**Field:** Mathematics  
**Institution:** Baku State University  
**Level:** 2nd Year Undergraduate Student  
**Date:** August 2026

## About

This independent research monograph presents a rigorous, self-contained development of selected advanced topics from the Mathematical Analysis curriculum.

The work develops the theory through precise definitions, theorems, proofs, and mathematical arguments, with an emphasis on classical real analysis and Euclidean space.

## Topics Covered

1. Uniform Convergence of Sequences of Functions
2. The Riemann Integral and Related Topics
3. Differential Calculus of Functions of Several Variables
4. Multiple Integrals
5. Fourier Series
6. Metric Spaces and Compactness
7. Ordinary Differential Equations: Existence and Uniqueness

The author aims to develop the selected topics carefully and completely, using direct and elementary arguments where possible while introducing more abstract viewpoints when appropriate.
